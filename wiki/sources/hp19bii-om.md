# HP 19BII Business Consultant II Owner's Manual

Edition 2 (February 1991; edition 1 was December 1989), part number
00019-90054. A 374-page scan with hpcalc.org's OCR as a text layer
(per-page text in `raw/manuals/text/hp19bii-om-en.txt`, block N = PDF
page N; the OCR drops most key glyphs and menu labels, so read them off
the page images). Public copy:
<https://literature.hpcalc.org/community/hp19bii-om-en.pdf>. HP's own
scan of the same manual (`/official/hp19bii-om-en.pdf`, 18 MB) has no
text layer and was not kept. Book page = PDF page - 4 in the body. The
body text calls the calculator "HP-19B" throughout; the covers say
19BII.

Read for [[hardware/hp19bii]]: chapter 1, chapter 8 (time and
appointments) and appendix A. The application chapters (TVM, cash flows,
bonds, statistics, Solver) were not read, hence status skimmed.

## Where the useful parts are

- **PDF p. 24 (book 20)**: power on and off, ten-minute auto-off,
  low-battery symbol, contrast (hold ON, press + or −), the language
  setting under MODES INTL.
- **p. 25 (21)**: Figure 1-1, the display: three lines over the menu
  labels and six annunciators (shift, alpha, past-due appointment, low
  battery, radians, printer), left to right.
- **p. 26-27 (22-23)**: Figure 1-2, the keyboard in two halves: the
  letter half with its printed reference card (MAIN menu, CLEAR, display
  contrast, other functions) and its shifted characters; the display
  half with its shifted labels and 25 numbered callouts.
- **p. 41 (37)**: available memory, "approximately 6,600" bytes, shown
  by MEM while the key is held.
- **p. 55 (51)**: degrees and radians in the MODES menu.
- **p. 179-185 (175-181)**: TIME menu: clock and calendar, SET (time,
  date, formats), ADJST, six appointments with alarms and messages,
  acknowledgement, past-due appointments.
- **p. 203 (199)**: printing and the print annunciator.
- **p. 279-283 (275-279)**: reset (ON + the third menu key, "MACHINE
  RESET"), clock accuracy, the self-test (ON + the fifth menu key; the
  fourth starts the factory test).
