# Garnier, Emu71 (DOS) web page

The page of Garnier's HP-71B emulator for MS-DOS (dated 2016 by
[[sources/hp71b-compendium]]). Web page, not in `raw/`; read on
2026-10-12 for its ROM statement only. Its URL is left out because the
page offers ROM images; search for Garnier's Emu71 page.

- The emulator uses images of the HP-71B ROM and of modules (Math, Forth,
  HP-IL and others), offered as a separate download.
- On permission, Garnier writes that he has no formal authorisation from
  Hewlett-Packard, but considers the images "consistent with the
  publication by HP of the source listings of these ROM", and that he
  would remove them at once if HP objected.

Used on [[hardware/saturn-models]] (ROM availability). No download
location is recorded in this wiki.
