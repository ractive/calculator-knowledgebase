# Wikimedia Commons photographs of the HP 19BII

Two photographs of an open HP 19BII on Wikimedia Commons, measured for
the case layout and sampled for approximate colours. Neither is stored
here or in saturnus; the facts drawn from them are on
[[hardware/hp19bii]] "Case and colours".

- **P1**: striegel, CC BY 2.0, the open calculator switched on, showing
  its self-test's pass message, taken square on.
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HP_19B_II_calculator,_open,_power_on,_showing_successful_self-test_(20191371831).jpg>
  Measured on its 1920 x 1567 rendering.
- **P2**: J. C. Barros's photograph, turned into a plan view with
  corrected colours by Pittigrilli, CC BY 2.0; used for colours and to
  check P1. It shows the language prompt with the menu labels D E F I P
  ENG.
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HP-19BII_Business_Consultant_II,_Financial_Calculator_from_Hewlett-Packard_(changed_to_plan_view,_improved_colours).jpg>

Colours from photographs depend on the light and the camera; take them
as approximate.
