# HP Graphing Calculator PC Connectivity Kit User Guide

20-page scan with OCR text, the same short guide in several languages; read
the English part (first 3 pages).

- One CD with two kits: one for the HP 48 series (and 49G), one for the HP
  38G. Windows 95 or later.
- Cable with a 9-pin PC end; a calculator-end adaptor is needed for the 48
  series and must **not** be used with the 49G or 38G.
- 48/49G kit: only baud rate and checksum need to match the calculator; the
  calculator's server must be started from its I/O functions; transfers by
  drag and drop on the PC.
- 38G kit: all transfers are started and controlled from the 38G; the PC
  side may need its "HP 38G directory" initialised from the calculator.
- The warranty states that the software on the CD is not HP software and is
  provided under the GNU General Public License.
