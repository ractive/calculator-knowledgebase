# comp.sys.palmtops HP100LX FAQ

The newsgroup FAQ for the HP 100LX and 200LX, last modified 1996-01-01,
read on 2026-11-08 (plain text). Web copy, not in `raw/`.

Sections read: 100LX vs 200LX vs 95LX (hardware the same, both MS-DOS 5.0
and CGA, the 100/200LX port is a standard serial port with hardware
handshaking), an MSD listing of a 1 MB 100LX (80186, one COM port at
0x3F8, 8250 UART), ROM versions with serial-number prefixes, the IR
transmitter, DataComm speed, section 12 (Connectivity Pack F1021A, cable
F1015A, adapter kit F1023A; the cable ends in a female DE9 with a null
modem), and "How fast is the highest speed for serial port?".

Reliability: a user FAQ; the speed answer is one view, contradicted by
other sources (see [[questions/hplx-serial-speed]]).
