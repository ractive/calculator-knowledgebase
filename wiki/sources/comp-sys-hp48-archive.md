# comp.sys.hp48 newsgroup archive (1990-2013)

The whole newsgroup as one mbox file (about 224,000 messages, 1990 to May
2013), part of the Internet Archive's "Usenet groups within comp" Giganews
collection: <https://archive.org/download/usenet-comp/comp.sys.hp48.mbox.zip>
(119 MB zip, 430 MB mbox). Not in `raw/` because of its size; downloaded once
on 2026-10-12 to a scratch directory and searched with a script. Google
Groups, which rate-limited the earlier attempt, was not needed.

Read for [[questions/custom-rom-for-saturn-calculators]]. Only the messages
below were read; the rest of the archive is unread. Usenet posts are
first-hand only where the poster did the work (an HP developer on HP's
code, an author on their own program); other posts are user reports and
are marked so where cited. Several posters worked for HP or its
Australian calculator division (ACO) at the time: Dave Arnett (HP
Corvallis, 48 ROM releases), Jean-Yves Avenard and Cyrille de Brébisson
(ACO, 49G system and boot code). Christoph Giesselink is the Emu48
maintainer; his posts are documentation of what he observed, not his code.

Cite as `(src: [[sources/comp-sys-hp48-archive]] C7)`; pages that cite many of them say once that "C7" means this page. Message-IDs let
anyone find a post in another archive.

## Searches that found nothing relevant

Subject and body searches for a Forth system on the 48 or 49 (1993, 1995,
1999 and 2009 requests, C39 among them) got no answer naming one. No
post was found of anyone running their own code from the reset address of
a 48 or 49, or of a non-HP system flashed onto a 49G. No post was found
reporting a 49G bricked by a bad system image (as opposed to a damaged
boot sector, C21, C24).
