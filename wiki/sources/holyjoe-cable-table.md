# Horn, The HP Cable Table

An undated table of HP's serial cables and adaptors for the 38G, 48
series, 49G and the palmtops, read on 2026-11-08. Web page, not in `raw/`.

Per part number: what it connects, both connectors, the 10-pin keying
(none, "small" for the 38G/49G, "WIDE" for the palmtops), ferrite bead,
country of manufacture and comments. The two palmtop cables (F1015-80002
to a PC, F1016-80002 to a Mac) carry the warning that they fit the 49G and
destroy it. The keying note explains the three 10-pin keyings.

Facts used on [[hardware/hp100lx-200lx]] and [[hardware/hp49g]].
