# Museum of HP Calculators, Saturn and HP-71 pages

Two undated exhibit pages, read 2026-10-12 through a headless browser:
"The Saturn Processor" (<https://www.hpmuseum.org/saturn.htm>) and
"HP-71" (<https://www.hpmuseum.org/hp71.htm>). Web pages, not in `raw/`.

- Saturn: first used in the 71B; the descendants are named Lewis (1 MHz,
  28C/S and high-end Pioneers; the 28C attribution conflicts with Emu28,
  see [[hardware/saturn-models]]), Clarke (2 MHz, 48S/SX), Yorke (4 MHz,
  48G/GX) and Bert (640 kHz, low-end Pioneers); the lower-end models put
  RAM and ROM on the processor chip.
- HP-71: QWERTY keyboard plus a numeric keypad; a one-line 22-character
  LCD that also works as an 8 x 132 dot graphics display; 64 KB ROM, 17.5
  KB RAM; four front ports; an optional hand-pulled card reader (HP-75
  cards); HP-IL through a plug-in module; a real-time clock and timers;
  HP published the OS documentation including its full source.

Used on [[hardware/saturn-models]].
