# HP 48SX Owner's Manual, volumes I and II

882-page scan of both volumes in one PDF, with hpcalc.org's OCR as a text
layer (per-page text in `raw/manuals/text/hp48sx-om-en.txt`; in that file
the block for a page is one further on than its PDF page number, because
the file's header is a block of its own). Public copy:
<https://literature.hpcalc.org/community/hp48sx-om-en.pdf>. The year is
that of the calculator's introduction; the edition's own date was not
looked up (unverified).

Read so far: appendix E "HP 48 System Flags" (book p. E-1 to E-7 = **PDF
p. 743-749**), from the text layer, with p. E-1 and E-2 checked against the
page images (the OCR drops the flag numbers -2 to -4 on E-1). Status
skimmed because the rest was not read for this page.

## Appendix E

- A table of all system flags -1 to -64 in functional groups, each with the
  meaning of clear and of set, or "not used" (-4, -13, -14, -27 to -29,
  -54). Transcribed in [[hardware/system-flags-48sx]].
- All flags default to clear except the word-size flags -5 to -10 (E-1).
