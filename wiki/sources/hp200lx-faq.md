# 200LX Frequently Asked Questions

A later, 200LX-oriented version of [[sources/palmtops-faq]], undated, read
on 2026-11-08 on the Retro Isle site. Web page, not in `raw/`.

Parts read: the connectivity answers (cable F1015A also used for HP's
calculators with an adapter; the cable ends in a female DB9 with a null
modem; adapter kit F1023A; Connectivity Pack F1021B/C; a 10-pin connector
"bump" against inserting it upside down) and "What software can I use":
the 200LX's DataComm supports XModem, YModem, ZModem and Kermit; MS-Kermit,
LapLink and Interlink work too.

Facts used on [[hardware/hp100lx-200lx]].
