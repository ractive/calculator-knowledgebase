# Wikimedia Commons photographs of the HP 28S

Two photographs of an open HP 28S on Wikimedia Commons, both taken
square on, measured for the case layout and sampled for approximate
colours (the median of a small patch). They are not stored in `raw/` and
nothing of them is reproduced here. Colours from photographs depend on
the lighting and white balance, so the hex values on the model page are
approximate.

- **P1**, open, power off, front, flat light on white; striegel (Flickr),
  CC BY 2.0, taken 2016-02-02. 2921 x 2379 px; measured on its 1920 x 1564 rendering,
  where the letter half spans 895 x 1521 px from (50, 25) and the
  display half 879 x 1488 px from (960, 47).
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HP-28S_calculator,_open,_power_off_(24687429911).jpg>
- **P2**, open, front; Ralf Pfeifer, CC BY-SA 3.0 de, taken 2009-11-02.
  698 x 587 px. Its EXIF gives 152 by 160 dpi, so its pixels may not
  be square: it serves for proportions along one axis and for colours.
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HP-28S.jpg>

Not measured: "HP-28S (2).jpg" (open, in dim light, colours too dark)
and "HP-28S (Thomas Conté).jpg" (not looked at).
