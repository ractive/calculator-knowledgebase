# Wikipedia, HP Saturn and the Saturn calculator articles

Web pages, not in `raw/`, fetched 2026-10-12: "HP Saturn", "HP-71B",
"HP-28C" (28C and 28S), "HP-19B", "HP-17B", "HP-27S", "HP-32SII",
"HP-10B". Secondary and uncited in places; used for model summaries and
cross-checked against the emulator manuals where possible.

- HP Saturn: chip generations with models and clocks: 1LF2 (1984: 71B,
  "HP-44A"); 1LK7 (18C, 28C, later 71B; 640 kHz, more instructions);
  Bert 1LU7 (10B, 20S, 21S; 640 kHz, 10 KB ROM, 256 bytes RAM, LCD
  driver on chip); Sacajawea 1LR3 (14B, 22S, 32S, 32SII; 640 kHz, 16 KB
  ROM, 512 bytes RAM, LCD driver); Lewis 1LR2 (17B, 17BII, 19B, 19BII,
  27S, 28S, 42S; 1 MHz, 64 KB ROM, LCD driver, memory controller);
  Clarke and Yorke for the 48/38/39/40/49. The 49g+, 48gII, 50g, 39g+,
  39gs and 40gs run an emulated Yorke on a Samsung ARM920T (S3C2410
  family) and are not Saturn hardware.
- HP-71B: first Saturn product; 64 KB ROM, 17.5 KB user RAM, one-line
  22-character LCD, four module ports, optional card reader and HP-IL;
  HP published IDS documents with the complete ROM source.
- HP-28C / 28S: 28C a Saturn at 640 kHz with 2 KB user RAM; 28S a Lewis
  at 1 MHz with 32 KB RAM; 137 x 32 dot LCD (four lines); no computer
  interface, IR printer only.
- HP-19B: clamshell, 1988-1990, 132 x 32 dot LCD (the 28S figure is
  137; neither is checked), IR printer; the 19BII followed in 1990.
- HP-17B / 17BII: Lewis, 64 KB ROM, 8 KB RAM, 131 x 16 dots, IR printer,
  clock with alarm. The 17bII+ (2003 and later) is not Saturn hardware.
- HP-27S: same internal hardware as the 17B: Lewis, 64 KB ROM, 8 KB RAM,
  131 x 16 dots, IR printer, 37 keys.
- HP-32S / 32SII: Saturn at 640 kHz, about 390 / 384 bytes for the user,
  twelve 5 x 7 character cells, no I/O.
- HP-10B: Bert, seven-segment LCD, 12 digits; the 10BII (Hitachi H8) and
  10bII+ (ARM) are not Saturn hardware.
