# HP 39G/40G User's Guide

288-page text PDF. Read: the annunciator table (ch. 1), "Downloading
e-lessons" and "Sending and receiving aplets" (p. 16-4, 16-5), and the
matrix/list send-receive notes in the Matrices and Lists chapters. No
protocol details appear anywhere in the guide (no mention of Kermit or
XModem).

- Aplets, programs, lists, matrices and notes can be sent to another HP 39G
  over IR (calculators no more than 2 in / 5 cm apart, triangle marks
  aligned) or by cable to "a remote storage device (aplet disk drive or
  computer)", which needs an aplet disk drive or PC software such as the
  Connectivity Kit (p. 16-5).
- The **HP 40G has no IR port**; it ships with a PC adapter and a
  unit-to-unit cable instead (p. 16-5).
- SEND and RECV each offer two destinations: another HP 39G, or a disk drive
  on a PC (p. 16-5). When receiving from the PC Connectivity Kit, the
  calculator lists the aplets in the PC's current directory and the user
  checks those to receive, i.e. the calculator drives the transfer (p. 16-5).
- Notes and sketches attached to an aplet travel with it (p. 16-4).
- A transmit annunciator shows "data is being transferred via infrared or
  cable" (ch. 1, annunciators).
