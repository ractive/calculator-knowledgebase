# Côté, HP-71B Compendium

A four-part catalogue of HP-71B products, documents and software in the
HP Museum articles forum (2015, revised 2020-08-31). Part 1 read on
2026-10-12 through a headless browser (lite archive view). Web page, not
in `raw/`.

- The 71B: 64 KB ROM and 17.5 KB RAM, 1985 list price; peripherals
  include the HP 82400A card reader, the HP 82401A HP-IL interface (16 KB
  ROM) and the HP 82402A dual HP-IL adapter; RAM and ROM modules from HP
  and third parties (HHP, CMT); later the FRAM71 module (2015).
- HP sold Internal Design Specifications: Volume I (detailed design),
  Volume II (entry points and poll interfaces, per ROM version 1BBBB and
  2CCCC), Volume III (the operating system source, per ROM version), a
  Hardware Design Specification, and an HP-IL IDS for the 82401A. Scans
  are part of the compendium.
- Part 1 lists ROM images of the 71B (operating system versions such as
  0AAAA and 2CCCC) and of its modules, contributed by Gießelink, Garnier,
  Frederickson and Prosperi. Côté states his understanding that, given
  their age, the documents and images may be shared for personal
  non-commercial use, and asks copyright holders to contact him if not.
  No HP permission is cited.

Used on [[hardware/saturn-models]]. No download location is recorded in
